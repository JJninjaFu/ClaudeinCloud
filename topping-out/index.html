Work order 021 · Steel frame, downtown lot

Topping Out

The foundation is poured. Now fly the steel. Pick pieces from the laydown yard, set them on the grid, and build the frame up floor by floor. Four floors tops out the job. After that, the sky is the limit.

You start on the gantry crane: it rides rails over the whole lot, moves in four directions with no swing, and jacks itself higher as your tower grows. Want a challenge? Switch to the tower crane, where loads swing.

1ColumnsStand on the grid points. Each one stacks on the column below it.
2BeamsSpan between two columns at the same height. TURN swings a beam 90°.
3DeckDrops into a square framed by four beams. A deck finishes a floor.

In a hurry? Tap the mode button until it says Mode: Hand, pick Column, Beam or Deck, and drag your finger across the site. A piece drops into every spot you pass over.

Green ghosts show where the piece on your hook can go. Picked up the wrong piece? Tap HOOK anywhere that is not a ghost and it goes back to the laydown yard.

PhoneLeft stick moves the hook around the lot (on the tower crane: swing and trolley). Right stick hoists (up/down) and looks around. HOOK picks up, sets, or sends back. TURN rotates a beam.
