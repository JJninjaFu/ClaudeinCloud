ClaudeinCloud

Browser games built with Claude. Each one runs on a phone, with a keyboard or with a controller.

  • Diggin' N Destructin'Isometric RTS with free-form terraforming. Deploy a base, harvest ore, and use excavators and engineers to dig trenches, pile berms and haul sand. 2000 credits to start.Command
  • Rippin' N Flippin'Stand-up jetski on real FFT water. Run the bay, launch off the set waves at the beach, pull back and stick the backflip.Ride
  • Skydivin' 24-way formation skydiving off a helicopter. Lean to fly your slot, build the shape, hold it 3 seconds. Up to 4 friends.Jump
  • Survivin' Skydivin'Pick your spot, pull on time, cut away a bad canopy, and land on the X.Jump
  • Funnin' N Gunnin'Red vs Blue, 25 a side, up to 4 friends. Jeeps, APCs and tanks. Dig foxholes, blow holes in walls.Fight
  • Mineral ForkDirt bike hillclimb up Trail 1154 and the red scree. Two mountains in two days.Ride
  • Pool DigExcavator sim: dig a backyard pool to grade and load the dump truck.Play
  • Dig & PourDig a 20 ft basement downtown, then pump the concrete mat.Play
  • Topping OutFly steel with a gantry or tower crane and build four floors.Play
