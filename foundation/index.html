Dig & Pour

The old building is gone and the lot is scraped flat. Before any steel goes up, this tower needs a basement and a foundation. Dig a 33 × 33 ft hole 20 ft deep, then pour a 3 ft thick concrete mat across the bottom.

1DigThe big excavator has laser grade control. It stops cutting at 20 ft, so you can't overdig. Two haul trucks take turns in the driveway.
2RebarWhen the floor is on grade, the ironworkers tie the steel mat. Sit back for a few seconds.
3PourSwing the pump hose over the hole and hold POUR. Concrete flows and levels out. Fill it to the dashed line.
