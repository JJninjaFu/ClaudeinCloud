How it works

Build a plane out of a body, an engine, wings and fins. Every wing panel makes its own lift and drag, so the shape you build decides how it flies.

  • Balance: the yellow ball is the weight, the blue ball is the lift. Keep yellow a little ahead of blue. Slide the wing back and forth to fix it.
  • Phone: left thumb is the stick (pull down to raise the nose). Slide the THR bar for power. Rud buttons steer on the ground. Tilt turns on tilt steering.
  • Keys: arrows = stick (down arrow raises the nose) · W/S throttle · A/D rudder · Space fire · F missile · G flare · X brake · C camera · T assist · R restart · B hangar
  • Controller: left stick · right stick rudder · RT/LT throttle · RB fire · LB missile · right stick click flare · A brake · Y camera · X assist · B restart · Start hangar
  • Take off: full throttle, wait for speed, ease the nose up. Assist steadies the plane and stops most stalls; turn it off for raw controls.
  • Dogfight: add guns in the hangar, pick how many bandits to fight, and press Fly. Put the circle on the yellow diamond and fire. Wings can be shot off, engines can be knocked out. Bandits leave you alone until you are off the ground.
  • Missiles: add them under Weapons. Keep a bandit near the circle until its box turns solid yellow (about a second), then fire. A missile chases heat but cannot turn as hard as a plane. When MISSILE flashes, drop flares and turn hard across its path. The round radar shows everyone within 2.5 miles, with you in the middle and your nose pointing up.
  • Friends: type a name, tap Host and send your friends the 4-letter code, or type their code and tap Join. Up to 4 pilots, each in a plane they built. Press Fly whenever you are ready; you can go back to the hangar, change planes and fly again without leaving the room.
  • With no bandits, fly through the yellow rings for a lap time. Land back on the runway if you can.
