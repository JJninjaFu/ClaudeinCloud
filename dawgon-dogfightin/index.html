How it works

Build a plane out of a body, an engine, wings and fins. Every wing panel makes its own lift and drag, so the shape you build decides how it flies.

  • Balance: the yellow ball is the weight, the blue ball is the lift. Keep yellow a little ahead of blue. Slide the wing back and forth to fix it.
  • Phone: left thumb is the stick (pull down to raise the nose). Slide the THR bar for power. Rud buttons steer on the ground. Tilt turns on tilt steering.
  • Keys: arrows = stick (down arrow raises the nose) · W/S throttle · A/D rudder · Space brake · C camera · T assist · R restart · B hangar
  • Controller: left stick · right stick rudder · RT/LT throttle · A brake · Y camera · X assist · B restart · Start hangar
  • Take off: full throttle, wait for speed, ease the nose up. Assist steadies the plane and stops most stalls; turn it off for raw controls.
  • Fly through the yellow rings for a lap time. Land back on the runway if you can.
