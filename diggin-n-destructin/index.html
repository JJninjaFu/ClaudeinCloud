Real-time strategy · move dirt, win wars

Diggin' N Destructin'

Deploy your construction vehicle, build a base, harvest ore and march on theirs. Every shovelful of ground is real: trenches, berms and craters change where soldiers hide and where tanks can go.

Mouse Left click or drag to select · right click to move, attack or harvest · wheel zoom · arrow keys or screen edge scroll · Q/E rotate · Ctrl+1-9 set a group, 1-9 recall · A attack-move · S stop · D deploy · H home
Engineers T then draw a line on the ground: they walk it and dig a trench with sandbags on the lips.
Excavators Z dig, X fill, C level: draw a line and the machine moves real dirt, scoop by scoop. What it digs it must dump somewhere; fill from the sand piles in your base. Trenches and berms stop vehicles.
Phone Tap a unit to select it, tap the ground to send it, drag to scroll, pinch to zoom. BOX switches dragging to box-select. Press and hold a sidebar item to cancel it. Menu hides the sidebar.
