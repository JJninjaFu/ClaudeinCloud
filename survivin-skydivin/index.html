Pick your spot, pull on time, handle whatever the canopy throws at you, and stick the landing on the X.

Controls & how to survive
  • Phone: left thumb anywhere on the left side is the stick. Big yellow button does the next thing (JUMP, PULL, KICK, CUT AWAY, RESERVE, hold to FLARE). Hold DIVE to go head down.
  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows steer · Space = big button · Shift dive · X cut away · R reserve · C camera · P pause · M sound
  • Controller: left stick · A big button · RT dive / flare · LT brakes · X cut away · Y reserve · B camera · Start pause
  • Exit on the green light. Freefall: push forward to track, back to backslide, sideways to turn. Fly through the rings.
  • Pull by 3,500 ft. Line twists: rock the stick left-right (or tap KICK). Streamer or pilot chute in tow: CUT AWAY then RESERVE, above 1,800 ft if you can.
  • Under canopy, pull down on the stick for brakes. Land into the wind (the windsock points downwind). Flare at about 10 ft; your shadow helps you judge.
