Skydivin' 2

Four jumpers off the skids of a helicopter at 13,500 ft. Lean forward and back to fly your slot, build the shape, hold it 3 seconds, then the next one. Break off at 4,500 ft and land on the X.

Docking
Controls & tips
  • Phone: left stick leans you forward/back (drive to and from your slot) and turns you. Right stick slides you sideways and controls fall rate (up = float, down = sink). Big button pulls and flares.
  • Keyboard: W/S lean · A/D turn · Q/E slide · R float · F sink · Space pull / flare · C camera · M sound
  • Controller: left stick lean & turn · right stick slide & fall rate · A pull / flare · B camera
  • Your ghost shows your slot. Get in it, face the right way, and match level (the bar on the right). When all four are docked, hold for 3 seconds.
  • You carry momentum, just like leaning on the bike: start braking (lean back) before you get there or you'll bump into your buddy.
  • At 4,500 ft: break off, push forward to track away, pull by 3,500 ft, then fly your canopy to the X.
